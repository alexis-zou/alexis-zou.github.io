About Me

Hi, I'm Alexis! I'm a first year CS student at Stanford interested in AI, product, tech ethics, and how technology can shape business and healthcare.

I got into research in high school, starting with student survey data at the Oregon Health Authority. Now I work in a computational neuroscience lab at OHSU, where I use Python to model how the auditory cortex responds to natural sounds. Recently, I also built Sencha, an app for running student-owned business pop-ups.

Outside of class, you'll find me at the piano or on the course with Stanford's competitive club golf team. I'm also actively involved with Stanford Women in CS.
